A few useful details

What photos and information do you need?

Clear photos you have permission to use, product names, your logo and colours if available, and confirmed prices or offers. We check photo quality before agreeing an order.

Do you create new product photography?

The service builds promotions around your existing photos. The fictional portfolio uses generated imagery for demonstration. We do not invent product features, ingredients, reviews, or performance claims.

What do I receive?

Final PNG images in square and story formats, plus captions in an editable text document. Editable Canva files are not included in the stated package.

When will my pack arrive?

We confirm an achievable delivery date after reviewing your photos and brief. Work begins after the agreed scope and payment are confirmed.

How do revisions and monthly renewals work?

Send all your requested changes together for the included revision round. Monthly work renews only when you choose to arrange the next month; there is no automatic card charge.
